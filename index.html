Counter loop

Bold arrows around the outside are direct counters. Intertwined (crossing) dotted arrows are light counters, and dotted lines marked NO ADV are flat, stuck matchups where neither side can do much to the other. Light counters and flat lines depend entirely on the type of brawler and the circumstance or situation.
